# Guest Feedback System for Eko Grand Hotel

A private feedback line on WhatsApp, alerts in under 60 seconds, and a Gold List of returning guests.

## The proposal at a glance
:::label Overview

This proposal is for a guest feedback system for **Eko Grand Hotel**. Guests scan a code, rate their stay in private, and the right manager hears about a problem while the guest is still in the building. Amara sets it up, trains the team and supports it for three months.

:::cards
### [hotel] 1 hotel | Scope
All 120 rooms, the restaurant and the spa.
### [clock] 5 weeks | Delivery
From kick-off to the team using it every day.
### [heart-handshake] 3 months | Support
Fixes and help after go-live, included.
### [shield-check] 100% | Ownership
Your guest list belongs to the hotel.
:::

:::takeaway A working demo comes with this proposal, so your managers can try a guest's journey before anything is signed.

## How a complaint reaches you
:::label The visual plan

:::flow
- Guest scans the code
- Rates privately
- **Amara alerts the manager**
- Manager replies
- Resolved before checkout
:::

The alert carries the guest's room, their rating and their own words, so the manager walks in knowing what happened.

## What Amara does
:::label Modules

:::hub Amara | your guest desk on WhatsApp
- Answers guest questions from the hotel's own information
- Logs every arrival and greets returning guests by name
- Flags unhappy guests before they check out
- Sends requests to the right department
- Collects feedback after every stay
- Builds the Gold List of guests worth bringing back
:::

## Implementation phases and timeline
:::label Delivery plan

The rollout runs for five weeks. Each phase ends with something the team can use.

:::phases
### Foundation | Week 1
- WhatsApp number connected
- Hotel information loaded
Outcome: guests get answers from day one.
### Connect | Weeks 2 to 3
- Check-Ins switched on
- Feedback after every stay
- Escalation routes set
Outcome: no unhappy guest leaves unheard.
### Grow | Weeks 4 to 5
- Gold List campaigns
- Monthly guest insights
Outcome: more guests come back.
:::
