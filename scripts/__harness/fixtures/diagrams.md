# Digital Church

From church information to a connected church.

## How a member **reaches us**

:::flow
- Member
- WhatsApp
- **Helpdesk**
- Knowledge base
:::

## What the helpdesk **does**

:::hub Helpdesk | answers on WhatsApp
- Answers questions
- Registers members for programmes
- Sends people to the right team
:::

## Build in **phases**

:::phases
### Foundation | 3 to 4 weeks
- WhatsApp line
- Church knowledge base
Outcome: a working information line.
### Connect | 3 to 4 weeks
- Branch knowledge
- Follow-up
Outcome: branches connected.
:::
