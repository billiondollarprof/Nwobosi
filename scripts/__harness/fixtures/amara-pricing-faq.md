# Amara Pricing FAQ

Everything you need to know before you sign up.

## What plans does Amara offer?

Three plans, each built for a different scale of business: **Essential**, **Premier**, and **Signature**.

## What does each plan cost, and what's included?

**Essential**, ₦52,200/month (₦563,760/year, billed annually, save 10%)
For smaller restaurants, guest houses and growing hospitality businesses.

- 300 messages a month
- Every rating in one inbox, plus a shareable review wall
- Guest suggestions

**Premier**, ₦66,600/month (₦719,280/year, billed annually, save 10%)
For growing restaurants, spas and lounges.

- Everything in Essential
- 450 messages a month
- Guest Insights: what guests wrote, and what they mention most
- Tracking of replies and unresolved issues
- The Gold List: six guest lists you can act on
- Exportable guest list, any time

**Signature**, ₦124,200/month (₦1,341,360/year, billed annually, save 10%)
The complete Amara experience, for hotels, shortlets, apartments, resorts and multi-location restaurants.

- Everything in Premier
- 1,000 messages a month
- Check-Ins: every arrival logged, returning guests recognised by name
- Connect your own system to send Amara every arrival and departure
- Up to 5 staff members

## Can I pay annually?

Yes. Annual billing is available on every plan and saves you 10% compared to paying monthly.

## Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You don’t need a card to create an account. A card is only needed once you activate a paid subscription.

## What is the 30 Day Feedback Guarantee?

For your first 30 days on a paid plan, if no guest has left feedback through Amara in that time, you can cancel and get a full refund. The refund goes back to the account the payment came from.

1. Contact our support team on the 30th day of your subscription.
2. Display your QR code where guests naturally pause.
3. Wait for the refund to arrive.

> Outside the guarantee, fees are non-refundable once a billing period has started.
