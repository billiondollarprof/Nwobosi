# StellaXafe: a private AI assistant for Dreef

Prepared for Dreef by Anthony Nwobosi, StellaXafe.

## In one paragraph

StellaXafe is a private AI assistant built for your company. Your staff ask
for a document in normal language. The assistant already knows your files,
asks a few short questions, confirms what it understood, and produces the
finished work in your format. Every fact points back to its source so a
person can check it. Your customers can ask it questions on WhatsApp, and it
answers from your approved information only. It runs inside your own private
cloud, and nothing is used to train a model.

## The problem you already have

Your best people are already using AI. The good ones got there first.

To get useful work out of a public chatbot, they paste in what makes the
work good: a client file, a contract, a price list, last year's report. A
tool that does not know your company gives weak work, and the deadline is
today. So they paste it in. Nobody was careless. The job needed the context.

Once that happens, you cannot see it, you cannot take it back, and no
contract covers it. A policy does not survive a deadline.

The second cost is quieter. Staff write a prompt, get something close, and
rewrite it. Then again. Each round costs time, and the result still does not
sound like your firm.

StellaXafe gives your staff the quality they were chasing, without the
paste.

## What your staff experience

1. They ask for what they need, the way they would ask a colleague.
2. The assistant asks three to five questions. Only the ones it cannot
   answer from your own files.
3. It reads the request back: what it will make, who it is for, which of
   your documents it will use. The staff member fixes anything wrong.
4. Only then does it write.
5. The finished file is saved to your own storage, and a download link
   appears in the chat.

Nobody learns prompt writing. The questions do that job, and because the
assistant already knows your company, there are few of them.

The staff screen has no settings to get wrong. They talk, and it works.

## It knows your company

Your admin approves the files the assistant may read: templates, past
projects, price lists, standards, policies. Setup is simple: upload a file,
give it a name and a line saying what it holds. When the assistant writes,
it uses your formats, your numbers and your wording. Staff do not paste any
of it in.

Nothing is trained on your data. The assistant reads an approved file when
it needs it. It does not absorb the file into a shared model, so nothing you
upload can turn up in anyone else's answer.

## You can check every answer

The assistant does not fetch information and dump it on you.

1. It gathers the information.
2. It checks it, and marks anything it could not confirm.
3. It shows you the source of each fact, as a file or a link, so you can
   open it and verify it in seconds.

If something is missing, it says so and tells you who in your company to ask
or where to look. It does not guess to fill the gap. Nothing unconfirmed
enters a document until a person approves it.

This matters where a wrong figure costs money or credibility: a cable
schedule, a tender price, a compliance report.

## Research from the internet, when you want it

If a task needs outside information, the assistant can research it online.
You stay in charge.

1. It names the sites it wants to look at and asks first.
2. It finds the details it needs.
3. It shows you those details with the link to each, and asks again before
   any of it goes into your document.

You never open a finished document and find data you did not approve.

## Your customers can use it on WhatsApp

The assistant also lives where your customers already are. They message your
WhatsApp number, and the assistant answers.

- **It answers only from what you approved**, and only about your business.
  If your admin has not put it in, the assistant does not know it.
- **It is a front desk.** Customers can ask questions. They cannot upload
  files, change what the assistant knows, or ask it to do staff work.
- **A person can step in at any time.** If a customer asks for a human, or
  asks something the assistant should not answer alone, it says so in the
  same chat and your team is notified. Your team replies from the place they
  already answer from. While they are in the conversation, the assistant
  stays quiet, and it comes back after a few minutes of silence.

Your customers get an instant answer at any hour. Your staff only see the
conversations that need a person.

## Your data stays protected

- **It runs in your own private cloud.** We build it inside a private AWS
  account that belongs to Dreef, in a VPC (a network space only your company
  uses). The assistant reaches the AI model over a private link inside that
  network, so that traffic does not cross the public internet.
- **Nothing you do trains a model.** Your chats and files stay yours.
- **Files are encrypted** in storage and while they travel.
- **Each staff member is separate.** One person's chats and notes are never
  shown to another.
- **Your admin controls what it can read.** A file that is not approved does
  not exist as far as the assistant is concerned.
- **No passwords.** Staff sign in with a one time code sent to their work
  email. There is no password to steal, forget or reset.

## Your files and your tools

The knowledge the assistant reads lives in your private AWS account. The
finished work goes back to where your team already keeps files, and staff can
save a file straight to their own Google Drive.

The assistant connects to the systems your team works in, so nobody has to
move. Examples are Google Drive, Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack, Notion and
Canva. We agree the exact list with you at the start, based on what you use.

## You are not locked to one AI model

The assistant is provider agnostic. It runs on the model that suits you, and
you can change models later without rebuilding. We recommend a model that
writes at the level of the leading models for a fraction of their running
cost. Your files, your rules and your workflow stay as they are.

## What you get back

- **Fewer retries.** The back and forth happens in a short chat before the
  document exists.
- **Less time per document.** Staff start from a request, not a blank page.
- **The same standard from everyone.** Your templates and your wording, no
  matter who asks.
- **Lower running costs** than a seat based AI subscription for every staff
  member, because you pay only for what is used.
- **An answer for your own clients.** When a client or auditor asks where
  your data goes, you can tell them.

## What it can do for Dreef

- **Tender responses and proposals**, built from your past bids, rates and
  company profile.
- **Progress and inspection reports**, in your format, with figures drawn
  from your project files and each one pointing to its source.
- **Method statements and safety documents**, from your own standards.
- **Client letters and internal memos**, in your house style.
- **Customer questions on WhatsApp**, answered at any hour from your approved
  information, with your team stepping in when it matters.

## How we deliver it

The architecture already exists and already runs. We are not starting from a
blank page, which is why delivery takes weeks, not months.

1. **Scope meeting.** We learn the tools you use, the documents your team
   writes most, and who will use it first.
2. **Foundation.** We open your private AWS account and build the private
   network, the database, the storage and the private link to the model.
3. **Build and connect.** We set the assistant up in your account and
   connect your tools.
4. **Your knowledge.** We load your files and templates, and test the full
   flow on a separate test copy.
5. **Pilot.** A small group of your staff uses it for real work, and we
   adjust.
6. **Go live.** Your team starts, with a short walkthrough. There is little
   to learn.

**Your team is using it within about three weeks of the scope meeting.**
WhatsApp goes live as soon as Meta approves your business number, which
usually takes one to two weeks and starts on day one.

**What we need from you:** a contact in your IT team (to approve access to
your Google, Slack or Notion), a sample of your documents and templates, and
the WhatsApp number you want customers to use.

## Investment

### Setup, one time

Setup is a one time fee. We set it after the scope meeting, because it
depends on the tools you use, how many we connect, and how many teams start
on day one. As a guide, it falls between **$15,000 and $35,000**.

### Running costs, monthly

You pay for what you use. There is no seat fee and no per user charge. The
screens your staff use are hosted on Cloudflare at no cost. Everything that
touches your data and the AI model runs inside your private AWS account.

For a team of about 20 people, 100 GB of files, around 300 documents and
2,000 customer WhatsApp messages a month:

| | Per month |
|---|---|
| AI model usage | about $33 |
| Database and server | about $15 to $30 |
| Private network link | about $15 |
| File storage | about $2 |
| **Total** | **about $65 to $80** |

Your bill follows your usage. A quieter month costs less, a busier one more.
You set a monthly budget and get an alert before you reach it. A new AWS
account also comes with up to $200 in AWS credits, which covers the first
months.

### Billing: your card or ours

- **On your own card.** AWS bills you directly. You pay the running costs
  and nothing else.
- **On our card.** We pay AWS, watch the billing for you, and send you one
  monthly invoice: the AWS bill line by line, plus a 20% management fee.

Either way the AWS account belongs to Dreef, and so does everything in it.

## Support and maintenance

**The first three months are included.** Fixes, updates and adjustments
while your team settles in.

**After that, a retainer.** We look after the system as part of your team:

- **Automatic health checks** run on a schedule. If something is wrong, we
  are alerted and it gets fixed, often before your team notices.
- **A separate test copy** of your system. Every change is tried there
  first, so your live system only gets changes that already work.
- **Updates and improvements** as your needs change, and new sources or
  connections when you add them.

The retainer is agreed with you, based on how much you use the system.
